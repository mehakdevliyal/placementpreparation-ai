1. Formal Recruitment Assessment Mode

Designed for formal placement evaluation. The AI examiner adaptively asks structured technical, domain, and logic questions.

  • Domain Selection: Software Engineering, Data Science, System Design, Academic Subjects, HR Fundamentals, etc.
  • AI Voice & Code Input: Use the interactive mic (or press Insert) to speak your answers or write code in the embedded Monaco editor.
  • Scoring & Feedback: Evaluated on technical correctness, clarity of reasoning, and domain vocabulary.
2. Skill Development & Practice Labs

Zero evaluation penalty space to practice spoken communication, exam preparation, and multi-language fluency.

  • Academic Viva: Oral exam drills for engineering, science, and college viva preparation.
  • FluentFlow: Practice conversational fluency in English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, and Hindi.
  • Debate AI: Engage in sharp reasoning exchanges on user-selected topics.
  • Healthy Convo: Low-pressure friendly chat to boost confidence and public speaking skills.
