Section 01
Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Interview Platform ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information provided by users ("you") while using our AI-powered mock interview and candidate assessment service.

By creating an account or using any feature of this platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree, you should discontinue use of the platform immediately.

Section 02
Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the platform. This includes:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and hashed password at registration. If you sign in via Google OAuth, your Google name, email, and profile picture are received from Google.
  • Resume uploads: PDF or document files you optionally upload for AI-based resume analysis. Files are stored securely and used only for analysis during your session.
  • Interview responses: Text answers you provide during interview sessions. These are sent to the Gemini AI API for evaluation and stored as part of your assessment record.
  • Assessment results: Your score, performance tier, domain, evaluation summary, and timestamp are stored in our database and linked to your account.
  • Session data: Temporary session identifiers, interview progress, and preferences stored server-side during your session.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers, or sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

Section 03
AI & Data Processing

This platform uses Google Gemini AI to generate interview questions, evaluate your responses, and produce assessment summaries. Your interview conversation and resume content are transmitted to Google's servers for this purpose.

By using this platform, you consent to your interview responses and resume being processed by Google Gemini AI. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles API data.

AI-generated evaluations are automated. They are not reviewed by human assessors and are intended for practice and self-improvement purposes only. Scores and feedback are not official placement decisions.

Section 05
Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secured relational database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard Werkzeug hashing (PBKDF2/SHA256) before storage.

We implement the following security controls:

  • HTTPS-only transmission in production environments (HSTS enforced).
  • HTTP security headers on all responses.
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax to mitigate XSS and CSRF risks.
  • File uploads are validated by type and size (10MB maximum). Only permitted extensions are accepted.
  • Access to all authenticated routes requires a valid server-side session.

Despite these measures, no internet-based system is completely immune to security risks. You are advised not to include highly sensitive personal information in your interview responses.

Section 08
Examination Integrity & Automated Proctoring Policy

To ensure fairness, authenticity, and strict adherence to placement evaluation standards, the platform employs an Automated Proctoring Security Layer during active examination sessions.

Window Focus & Tab-Switch Restrictions (2-Strike Policy):

  • Continuous Session Focus: Candidates are required to maintain active window focus on the examination studio throughout the duration of the interview.
  • Strike 1 (Warning): The first instance of tab switching, window minimization, or application focus loss triggers an immediate warning dialog reminding the candidate of anti-cheating regulations.
  • Strike 2 (Immediate Termination): A second focus loss violation results in automatic session termination. The attempt is immediately recorded as Terminated (Code of Conduct Breach) with a score of 0/10.
Audit Logging: All security violations and premature session terminations are permanently logged in administrative audit records and displayed on candidate performance reports.
